06 · Source Key

How to read the guide

Relic recipe rewards come in several different forms.
Selection / Selector

You choose the Combat Engraving recipe. Highest practical value when you need a specific book.

Random Pouch / Chest

Gives a random Relic Combat Engraving recipe. Tradability depends on the exact reward container.

Direct RNG Drop

The activity can drop a Relic recipe directly or through a reward system such as Fate Embers.

Bound or tradable? Check the exact item tooltip before opening. A roster-bound book can be used across your roster, but cannot be sold. A selection pouch is not automatically tradable.
Use selectors carefully. Compare prices for the engravings your roster actually uses. Spend bound selectors on a needed, expensive book; check existing engraving progress before selecting.
Find the source in-game: open the Item Codex (Alt+D by default), search the exact recipe or pouch name, and inspect its acquisition sources. For books, use the Market’s Engraving Recipe category and Relic rarity filter. Legendary engraving rewards do not count as Relic books.
